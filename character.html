Characters of 東京リベンジャーズ

Takemichi Hanagaki

Takemichi is the main protagonist of the story. He gains the ability to travel back in time and tries to save his friends and change the tragic future.

Manjiro Sano (Mikey)

Mikey is the founding leader of the Tokyo Manji Gang. He is extremely strong and respected by his friends, but he also struggles with loneliness and darkness.

Ken Ryuguji (Draken)

Draken is the vice leader of the Tokyo Manji Gang and Mikey's closest friend. He is strong, calm and often helps Mikey make the right decisions.

Izana Kurojawa

Izana is the former leader of Tenjiku and one of the most dangerous fighters in the series. He is extremely skilled in combat and has a complicated relationship with Mikey.

Shinichiro Sano

Shinichiro is Mikey's older brother and the founder of the Black Dragons. Although he was not a great fighter, he was respected for his kindness, leadership and strong character.

Hinata Tachibana

Hinata is Takemichi's childhood girlfriend and one of the most important people in his life. She is kind, brave and strongly supports Takemichi throughout his journey.

Tetta Kisaki

Kisaki is one of the main antagonists of the series. He is highly intelligent, manipulative and willing to use others to achieve his goals. He plays a major role in many tragic events.

Keisuke Baji

Baji is one of the founding members of the Tokyo Manji Gang and a close friend of Mikey. He is fearless, loyal and extremely strong, always willing to sacrifice himself for his friends.

Shuji Hanma

Hanma is a powerful and unpredictable fighter who works closely with Kisaki. He enjoys chaos and fighting, and his mysterious personality makes him one of the most dangerous characters.
